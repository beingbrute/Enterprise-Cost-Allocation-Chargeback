# Halden Group Travel & Expense Policy

Fictional policy written for this project. All amounts in USD.

| Version | Effective | Changes |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | 1 Jan 2025 | First version |
| v2 | 1 Jul 2025 | Hotel caps raised; submission deadline shortened from 90 to 60 days |

## 1. Scope

Applies to all employees of Halden Group's five business units. Covers business travel, meals, client entertainment, training and small purchases paid personally and claimed back.

## 2. Limits

| Category | Rule | v1 | v2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hotel, standard city | Per night | $200 | $230 |
| Hotel, high-cost city (London, New York, Singapore, Mumbai) | Per night | $300 | $325 |
| Meals, travelling alone | Per day | $75 | $75 |
| Client entertainment | Per attendee | $150 | $150 |
| Gifts to clients | Per gift | $100 | $100 |
| Airfare | Economy class. Business class only with pre-approval | | |
| Receipts | Required for every item of $25 or more | | |
| Submission | Within this many days of the expense date | 90 days | 60 days |

## 3. Approvals

Every claim is approved by someone other than the claimant, at the level set by the claim total:

| Claim total | Approver |
|---|---|
| Under $500 | Line manager |
| $500 to $2,500 | Department head |
| Over $2,500 | Business unit finance director |

Splitting one expense into several claims to stay under an approval level is not allowed.

## 4. Not reimbursable

Alcohol unless clients are present, personal items, traffic and parking fines, upgrades without pre-approval, and gifts over the limit.

## 5. Policy exceptions

A manager can request a one-off exception before the expense (for example business class on a long-haul flight for medical reasons). Approved exceptions are recorded in the exception register with an expiry date. A claim covered by a valid exception is not a breach.

## 6. Policy checks

Every claim is checked automatically when it is submitted.

| Code | Check | Severity |
|---|---|---|
| P01 | Receipt missing for an item of $25 or more | Low |
| P02 | Hotel over the nightly cap for the city | Medium |
| P03 | Meals over the daily cap | Low |
| P04 | Business class or upgrade without pre-approval | Medium |
| P05 | Client entertainment over the per-attendee limit, or no attendees or business purpose recorded | Medium |
| P06 | Duplicate claim (same employee, date, merchant and amount) | High |
| P07 | Split claim: same employee, merchant and date, each part under an approval level but together over it | High |
| P08 | Approved at the wrong level, or approved by the claimant | High |
| P09 | Submitted after the deadline | Low |
| P10 | Item not reimbursable | High |

## 7. Cases

Each failed check opens a **case**.

1. **Intake.** The case is logged with the claim, the check, the amount in question, severity, owner and due date.
2. **Triage.** The case is assigned to a reviewer within 1 working day. High cases over $1,000 are escalated to the Manager at intake.
3. **Review.** The reviewer collects evidence (receipt, approval record, business purpose, attendee list, pre-approval) and decides:
   - **No breach:** the evidence shows the claim is within policy
   - **Exception approved:** a valid reason, recorded in the exception register
   - **Partly reimbursed:** the amount over the limit is disallowed
   - **Rejected:** the whole claim is disallowed
   - **Corrected:** the breach was in the process, not the spend (for example the wrong approver); the claim is re-approved correctly and nothing is disallowed
4. **Remediation.** For disallowed amounts: recovery from the employee (repayment or payroll deduction). For repeat or serious breaches: retraining or a notice to the business unit finance director.
5. **Closure.** A case closes only when the evidence for its decision is attached and any recovery is confirmed. A case is **reopened** if a recovery fails.

### Deadlines (working days)

| Severity | Decision due | Remediation due after decision |
|---|---|---|
| High | 3 | 20 |
| Medium | 5 | 20 |
| Low | 10 | 20 |

Cases past a due date are escalated to the Manager at the daily check. An employee with 3 or more cases in 12 months is reported to their business unit finance director.

## 8. Monthly governance controls

| Control | Passes when |
|---|---|
| G01 | Every claim submitted in the month was checked |
| G02 | Every case opened in the month was triaged within 1 working day |
| G03 | No case closed without the evidence its decision needs |
| G04 | Every past-due case was escalated |
| G05 | Amounts disallowed = amounts recovered + amounts still outstanding |
| G06 | Governance report issued for the month |
